# DOT//CORE Alpha 0.8 — Economy & Hidden Cheats

- Normal economy enabled: upgrades now spend credits.
- Starting credits: 120 C; passive income starts at 1 C/s.
- Rebalanced early/mid/late upgrade prices for finger, turrets, drones, effects and debuffs.
- Debuffs are intentionally cheaper and increase passive income; enemy density now has an effect from the first level.
- Enemy growth from bosses/debuffs softened to make the risk/reward curve more controllable.
- First purchased turret is placed at the exact center of the defense line.
- Hidden easter egg: tap the word `DOT` in the main title 10 times within the tap window to toggle free-purchase cheat mode. Another 10 taps disable it.
- Cheat mode persists in global settings and reproduces the old test behavior: free purchases/repairs and late-game wave gates bypassed.
