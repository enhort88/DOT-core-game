# Alpha 0.11 progression draft

This is intentionally an alpha tuning sheet. Unlock thresholds are designed to reveal only the next horizon.

## Finger
1. Tap Damage — visible from start.
2. Plasma — unknown technology; purchase becomes available at Tap Damage Lv.20. Tap upgrades use a deliberately gentle early price curve; Plasma then costs 1,200 C.
3. Roscherk — after Plasma Skill Lv.5. Cost: 2,600 C.
4. Annihilation — after Roscherk Skill Lv.8. Cost: 9,000 C.
5. Gravity — after Annihilation Skill Lv.8 + 10 bosses. Cost: 35,000 C.

## Turrets
Visible from start:
- Buy Turret
- Damage
- Fire Rate
- Shield
- Repair Skill status (levels by repairing, not by buying)

Discovery chain:
1. Pulse Laser — Damage Lv.8 + Fire Rate Lv.6. 3,000 C.
2. Rockets — Turret Skill Lv.6. 5,200 C.
3. Auto Repair — Repair Skill Lv.8 + 3 bosses. 9,500 C.
4. +2 turret slots — 10 bosses. 45,000 C.

## Drones
Visible from start:
- Gun Drone
- Damage
- Fire Rate

Discovery chain:
1. Missile Drone — Drone Skill Lv.4. 1,600 C unlock/purchase.
2. Support Drone — Drone Skill Lv.7. 2,600 C.
3. Kamikaze — 3 bosses. 3,200 C to discover; later spawns are cheap.
4. Aura — Drone Skill Lv.10. 5,000 C.
5. +2 drone slots — Aura Lv.5 + 10 bosses. 48,000 C.

## Elements
- Effect system button appears after boss 5.
- Fire: first discovery, 1,200 C.
- Ice: next discovery after boss 7, 2,400 C.
- Lightning: next discovery after boss 9, 4,800 C.
- Gravity is not in the Effect Shop; it stays the final Finger technology.

## Cheat/test mode
Ten taps on `DOT` toggles test cheats. While active:
- credits are effectively infinite;
- purchases cost 0;
- all Finger technologies are usable;
- all turret weapons / Auto Repair / late slots are exposed;
- all drone technologies and Aura are exposed;
- Effect Shop / all elements are available regardless of boss count.
