# DOT//CORE Alpha 0.5 — Combat Feel & Build Tuning

- Plasma hold now renders a growing energy charge around the finger with inward particles.
- Finger trail persists for about one second, stays animated in the air, and continues damaging enemies that touch it.
- Enemy movement speed now grows after each defeated boss.
- Added purchasable Enemy Speed difficulty upgrade; it also raises passive income.
- Added shop sell mode. Eligible purchases can be sold back for 60% of their estimated purchase price.
- Effect setup now contains both turret elemental charge and turret weapon selection; weapon cycling was removed from the shop.
- Save slots now show last reached wave and actual earned credits more clearly.
- Test mode no longer overwrites save credits with 999,999,999; purchases remain free and HUD still shows C ∞.
- Drone base cap increased from 7 to 9; late-game +2 slots now raise the cap to 11.
- Drones use individual wide patrol orbits instead of clustering on one shared ellipse, making aura positioning meaningful.
- Turrets now visibly rotate and recoil while firing; turret and drone silhouettes were redrawn.
- HUD controls, credit text and planet integrity meter were enlarged; planet HP was redesigned as a segmented bottom energy bar.
- Existing tap-to-collect battlefield bonuses remain enabled.
