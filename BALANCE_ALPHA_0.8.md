# DOT//CORE Alpha 0.8 — first economy pass

## Early game targets

- New save starts with **120 C** and **1 C/s** passive income.
- No starting turret. Basic tap remains sufficient to kill the first enemies.
- First turret costs **300 C**, so active play should unlock it early instead of giving it away.
- First tap upgrades cost 70/90 C, letting the player choose immediate active power instead of saving for the turret.

## Upgrade curves

- General damage: 120 C, growth x1.58.
- General attack speed: 150 C, growth x1.62.
- Enemy reward: 180 C, growth x1.60.
- Tap damage: 70 C, growth x1.52.
- Tap speed: 90 C, growth x1.55.
- Turrets: 300 C, growth x1.90 per installed turret.
- Turret damage: 180 C, growth x1.56.
- Turret attack speed: 220 C, growth x1.60.
- Gun drones: 700 C base, growth x1.48 with total drone count.

## Ability unlocks

- Plasma: 550 C.
- Trail: 850 C.
- Fire: 600 C.
- Ice: 900 C.
- Lightning: 1500 C.
- Turret laser: 1800 C.
- Turret rockets: 3200 C.
- Ultimate: 3800 C.
- Auto-repair: 5200 C (wave gate remains unless cheats are enabled).
- Gravity: 15000 C (wave 10 gate).
- +2 turret slots: 30000 C (wave 15 gate).
- +2 drone slots: 32000 C (wave 15 gate).

## Debuffs / risk-reward economy

Debuffs intentionally start cheaper because the player buys additional danger. Every debuff increases passive income.

- Density: 70 C, growth x1.55, +0.35 C/s per level.
- Spawn rate: 80 C, growth x1.56, +0.40 C/s per level.
- Enemy value: 100 C, growth x1.58, +0.45 C/s per level and larger kill rewards.
- Enemy speed: 90 C, growth x1.58, +0.50 C/s per level.
- Enemy damage: 95 C, growth x1.60, +0.55 C/s per level.
- Enemy HP: 95 C, growth x1.60, +0.55 C/s per level.

Enemy density now affects spawn batches from level 1 instead of only becoming meaningful at high levels.

## Selling

Purchased upgrades return **60%** of their last purchase value when sold.

## Hidden cheats

Cheats are OFF by default. Tap **DOT** in the main title 10 times to toggle free-purchase mode; repeat 10 taps to disable it.
