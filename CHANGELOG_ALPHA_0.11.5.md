# DOT//CORE Alpha 0.11.5 — Glass UI Polish

- Removed heavy full-colour fills from Store, Debuffs, Effect Store and Effect Setup.
- Fixed Android rendering mismatch caused by relying on ShapeRenderer alpha for shop surfaces.
- Shop surfaces now use explicit dark RGB glass tones; cyan/gold/red are accents only.
- Active tabs use a thin underline and faint dark glow instead of a cyan slab.
- Purchase cards are flatter and darker with a 3px accent rail and subtle selected edge.
- Debuff categories are neutral dark bands with small coloured group markers.
- Effect Setup Finger/Turret/Drone sections no longer use full cyan/gold backgrounds.
- Footer actions (Sell/Close/Effect Store/Back) are outline-style dark controls instead of solid coloured buttons.
- Mechanics, swipe navigation, Back behaviour and drone AI from 0.11.4 are unchanged.
- Version bump to 0.11.5-alpha.
